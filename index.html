    Mechanics, assumptions & source

    Clean DPS is the weapon/stat baseline. Matchup DPS uses the selected loadouts, target accuracy and opening defense roll. Armor-break estimates use the opening wear rate and do not simulate gear transitions or fatigue pauses.

    Defend bypasses the weapon block cooldown; it does not remove Dodge's own recovery state. Successful dodges spend stamina and may charge the attacker the intent's miss cost. Heavy weapons increase block fatigue with a positive STR gap, but are easier for a faster opponent to dodge. Swift-balanced weapons can suppress defense rolls with a speed lead; Swift attack stance is not selected in this Defend matchup.

    The race tracks expected fatigue and approximates dodge cooldown and roll degradation on an average trajectory. It assumes room to dodge and both fighters promptly return to range. No HP death, Guard/Clash, shield or weapon breakage, injuries, tempo, or ranged attacks. The armor catalog uses source-defined starting ratings and coverage. Runtime changes such as visor toggles, spells, and natural armor are not modeled.
