Mechanics, assumptions & source

Raw DPS assumes every hit lands on an unarmored target, a pristine weapon, normal attack stance, standing, and healthy arms. It excludes dodge, armor, wounds, criticals, quality, enchantments, special grips, traits, tempo and weapon binding. Only source-defined zero-charge, zero-windup melee intents are offered. It is not sustained combat DPS.

Class skills come from subclass_skills, not conditional outfit choices or training. Override the skill when your loadout grants a higher rank. Azure uses the best scaled primary/secondary skill. Armor checkboxes and enemy stats are explicit scenario assumptions, not inferred equipment.

Parry is the roll chance per eligible incoming attack, facing a player-like armed enemy, with an empty offhand (or two-handed grip), enough stamina, no parry cooldown, no impairments and no special traits. It is not overall avoidance or an outgoing hit probability. Swift attackers use SPD difference reduced by PER/INT according to target zone; only Ratwood grants the old martial +20 bonus.

Stamina is a fatigue budget; energy is the long-term reserve. Regen shown is fatigue recovered per eligible mob update after more than 2 seconds without spending stamina, at the chosen fixed energy percentage. Short-burst attack count starts fresh and ignores interruptions, incoming parries and regeneration. Each recovery update itself resets the fatigue timestamp, so recovery is not continuous every tick. WIL increases the pool, not the baseline tick amount at a fixed energy percentage.
