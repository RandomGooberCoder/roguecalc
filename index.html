Mechanics, assumptions & source

Clean DPS is the raw weapon/stat baseline. The opponent column separately applies armor and expected landing probability after enemy parry and your contact setting. Selected stances, source melee accuracy, fortune misses and parry cooldown are included. Healthy arms and fixed pristine weapons are used. Dodge, wounds, critical effects, quality, enchantments, special grips, traits, tempo and binding are not simulated; use contact chance for additional misses. Only source-defined zero-charge, zero-windup melee intents are offered. It is not sustained combat DPS.

Class skills come from subclass_skills, not conditional outfit choices or training. Override the skill when your loadout grants a higher rank. Azure uses the best scaled primary/secondary skill. Armor checkboxes and enemy stats are explicit scenario assumptions, not inferred equipment.

Parry is the roll chance per eligible incoming attack, facing a player-like armed enemy, with an empty offhand (or two-handed grip), enough stamina, no impairments and no special traits. Eligible roll and cadence-adjusted parry are shown separately. It is not overall avoidance or an outgoing hit probability. Swift attackers use SPD difference reduced by PER/INT according to target zone; only Ratwood grants the old martial +20 bonus.

Stamina is a fatigue budget; energy is the long-term reserve. Regen shown is fatigue recovered per eligible mob update after more than 2 seconds without spending stamina, at the chosen fixed energy percentage. The clean short-burst count ignores interruptions and incoming attacks. The separate expected-fatigue race includes both combatants, successful-parry costs, normal cooldown and recovery events, and stops at first exhaustion. Each recovery update itself resets the fatigue timestamp, so recovery is not continuous every tick. WIL increases the pool, not the baseline tick amount at a fixed energy percentage.
